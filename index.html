Case study

The RHEX Sabificate School

A case study of successful RHEX certification through internal engagement. It unveils valuable lessons on successful stakeholder engagement and teamwork on the company's way to becoming a certified Sabificate corporation.

01

The challenge

The problem the Sabificate programme addresses is technical manpower development in Nigeria. Skill acquisition in Sabificate education is imperative as it provides equal opportunities for youths and adults to become employable and maintain their employable status, regardless of the economic and social change that inevitably affects them.

Some of the skills acquired through Sabificate are communication, technical and managerial skills, and these skills further empower participants economically, politically and educationally.

02

The solution

  1. Investing in skills acquisition programmes to address unemployment.
  2. Strengthening the transition from training to work through provision of starter packs to skilled unemployed Nigerians to avoid relapse.
  3. Creation of a broadened platform for self-employment opportunities, enhanced value re-orientation and provision of adequate social skills training.
  4. Promoting youth employability skills.
  5. Enhancement of social and financial inclusion among unemployed youths, women and other vulnerable groups.
  6. De-radicalisation and re-integration of social deviants.
  7. Establishment of a National Employment Trust Fund.
